# Commercial Handoff

Fleet Drive AI is the intended commercial fleet-management product. Fleet Drive AI Demo is the working full-stack demonstration described in this repository.

## Proven by the Demo

- Real account registration/login and authenticated application access.
- Persistent vehicle, driver, maintenance, alert and geofence workflows.
- Simulated telemetry flowing through Express and SQLite into React/Leaflet.
- Deterministic route ordering and maintenance rules labelled as Demo logic.
- Report generation from stored maintenance and trip/history data.

## Required before a commercial product

- Production telematics/hardware adapters, device identity and ingestion security.
- Tenant isolation, administrative authorization, audit logs and account lifecycle.
- Managed relational storage, backups, migrations and retention policies.
- Queueing/streaming, observability, incident response and uptime objectives.
- Provider-backed notifications, road-network routing and validated export services.
- Security review, privacy/legal review, secret rotation and dependency maintenance.
- Commercial pricing, billing, support terms and deployment operations.
- Separately validated mobile apps, SSO and trained AI/ML only when genuinely implemented.

No Demo record, fictional scenario or visual should be presented as a customer, contract, production deployment, hardware integration, published mobile app, uptime claim or genuine testimonial.
